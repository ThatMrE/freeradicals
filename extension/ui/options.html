Free Radicals

Feeds stay closed until you publish something. A post buys you one timed window; when it ends, the feed closes and the next window costs another post.

You're set up

  1. Open a social feed — it will be covered by the block screen.
  2. Write something real and press Post & open feed.
  3. Scroll for your window. When it hits zero, the feed closes again.
